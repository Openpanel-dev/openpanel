## What it is

Every call your product makes to a language model becomes an event in OpenPanel: which model, how many tokens, what it cost, how long it took, whether it failed, and, if you want, the messages in and out. Calls are grouped into **traces** (one request) and optionally **conversations** (one thread).

What sets it apart from a standalone LLM tool is that the event lands next to everything else you track. An AI call belongs to a **profile**, happens inside a **session** and can carry **groups**, so you can answer questions like:

- What does our AI feature cost per user, per plan or per customer account?
- Which model or feature burns the most tokens, and where does latency spike?
- Do users who hit the assistant convert more often? That is a funnel with an `ai_generation` step.
- What did this user see on the page right before and after the model call? Open the session.

## What you get

- **AI section** in the dashboard with an overview (cost, generations, users, cost per user, tokens, p95 latency, error rate, all with previous-period comparison), cost by model, feature, provider, user and group, a **traces** list with a timeline per trace, and a **generations** list with the conversation of each call.
- **Reports**: AI events are ordinary events named `ai_generation`, `ai_embedding`, `ai_span` and `ai_trace` with flat `ai_*` properties, so the report builder, funnels, retention, cohorts and dashboards work on them. Sum `ai_cost` by `ai_model`, or take the p95 of `ai_latency_ms` by `ai_name`.
- **Profiles, sessions and groups** show an AI usage widget with lifetime cost, tokens, latency and errors.
- **Cost** is computed for you from a built-in price catalog covering the major providers. Send your own `cost` when a gateway already knows it.

## How to send events

| Your stack | Use |
|---|---|
| Vercel AI SDK (any provider) | [`@openpanel/ai/vercel` middleware](/docs/ai-observability/vercel-ai-sdk), one line per model |
| Node with our SDK | [`op.ai(payload)`](/docs/ai-observability/manual-capture) |
| Anything else (Python, Go, curl) | [the raw payload](/docs/ai-observability/manual-capture#raw-payload) on `POST /track` |

## Privacy

Prompts, completions and tool definitions are stored for 30 days, then dropped; tokens, cost and latency are kept with your other events. Set `privacyMode: true` on a call to never store its content, or turn content storage off for the whole project under **Settings → AI**.
