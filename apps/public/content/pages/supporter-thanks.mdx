Hey there! 👋

Thank you so much for becoming an OpenPanel supporter. It means a lot to us that you chose to back the project, and it really did make our day.

## What happens now

1. **Check your inbox.** You should have an invite to the private supporter channel on Discord. That is where you get priority help and can talk to the team directly. Nothing there? Email [hello@openpanel.dev](mailto:hello@openpanel.dev) and we will sort it out.
2. **Switch to the latest images.** Follow the [supporter setup guide](/docs/self-hosting/supporter-access-latest-docker-images) to log in to our registry and run `openpanel upgrade --supporter`.
3. **Tell us what to build.** Post ideas and vote on the [feedback board](https://feedback.openpanel.dev). Supporter requests get looked at first.

Running an open-source project takes a lot of time, and supporters like you make it possible to keep improving OpenPanel for everyone who self-hosts it.

Thanks again for believing in what we are building. You rock! 🚀
