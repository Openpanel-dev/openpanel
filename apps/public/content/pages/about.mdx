Hey there! My name is [Carl-Gerhard Lindesvärd](https://x.com/CarlLindesvard), and I started OpenPanel.

I have worked with analytics tools for years, mostly Mixpanel alongside Google Analytics. Here is why I decided to build my own.

## The problem

**Product analytics cost too much for what I used.** Mixpanel is a solid tool, but I only needed a small part of it: my custom events, a few [funnels](/features/funnels) and [retention](/features/retention). I was paying $300 a month for features I barely touched.

**Web analytics lived somewhere else.** For page views, visitors and referrers I needed a second tool, and keeping two analytics stacks in sync is a headache.

**Privacy was an afterthought.** Google Analytics has been found to break GDPR in several EU countries, and many tools store data in the US by default. Being based in Stockholm, I wanted something that took privacy seriously from day one.

## What I built

OpenPanel combines the depth of Mixpanel with the simplicity of Plausible. Web analytics, product analytics, [revenue](/features/revenue-tracking) and AI usage live in one place, without cookies, hosted in the EU or on your own servers.

It started as a side project in late 2023. Today thousands of projects use it, and I use it myself every day.

## Why open source

Software gets better when people can see it, run it and change it. Every line of OpenPanel is [on GitHub](https://github.com/Openpanel-dev/openpanel) under the AGPL license. Self-host it for free, read the code, or send a pull request. There is no enterprise edition hiding the good parts.

## Not the server control panel

**OpenPanel.dev** is product and web analytics. If you were looking for OpenPanel the web hosting control panel, an alternative to cPanel or Plesk, that is a different product from a different company.
